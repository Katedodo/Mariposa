Three references.

We are not copying a competitor’s look. We are identifying what builds desire, trust and an easy purchase — then combining it with Mariposa’s own advantage.
DESIRELacy Bird

WHAT WORKSA recognisable house style: editorial photography, soft palettes, generous floral shapes and a consistent premium point of view.

FOR MARIPOSACreate a distinctive image language, signature product names and photography that makes the catalogue feel curated rather than generic.

CLARITYWarsaw Flowers

WHAT WORKSA direct, product-first shopping model gives customers the familiar basics: visible bouquets, prices and a clear route to purchase.

FOR MARIPOSAKeep the catalogue easy to scan with useful filters, clear product cards, size options and pickup information without sacrificing personality.

TRUSTFlower Bar

WHAT WORKSStrong reassurance through visible prices, secure payment options, pickup and delivery information, customer reviews and clear service promises.

FOR MARIPOSAShow availability, pricing and checkout steps upfront; add social proof as it grows and make every order feel predictable and safe.

07 · PRACTICAL DETAILSLAUNCH PLAN

KEEPING THE FIRST STEP SIMPLE

Pickup first.
Delivery later.

01Complete store at launch

Customers browse the catalogue, filter products, add to cart and pay online.

02Pickup first

At checkout, customers select an available date and time to collect their order from Mariposa.

03Delivery later

Delivery is not required for the first launch. We can develop and connect it later, after Mariposa signs agreements with local delivery companies.

04Ready to expand

The store will be structured so delivery zones, prices and schedules can be added in the next phase without rebuilding the sales flow.
