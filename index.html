I’ll keep this simple

Payments, pickup
and what you need.

PAYMENTSChoose the route that suits you

You can connect Stripe or a Polish provider such as Przelewy24, PayU or Tpay. They can offer cards, BLIK, fast bank transfers and digital wallets.

If you prefer direct bank acquiring, you will need to agree the service and fees with your bank first. I will then integrate the solution they provide.

WHAT YOU DOOpen and verify the merchant account

You provide your business and owner details, sign the provider agreement and add your business bank account for payouts. I take care of the website integration and testing.

DELIVERYStart with pickup. Add delivery later.

At launch, customers book a pickup time in the calendar. Later, after you agree terms with a local courier, we can add delivery zones, prices and schedules.

External costs: domain, hosting, provider fees, photography, optional paid tools and future courier charges.
